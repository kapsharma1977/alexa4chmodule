Power Supply Recommendations
	*Use a 1A or 2A Supply: A standard 5V 2A SMPS adapter is highly recommended to provide enough "headroom" for the WiFi spikes and all four relays.
	*Filtering Capacitors: If you experience random resets when the relays click, adding a large electrolytic capacitor (e.g., 470µF or 1000µF) across the 5V and GND pins can help smooth out the voltage dips caused by these sudden current demands.
	*Avoid USB-only Power: Standard computer USB ports are often limited to 500mA, which may not be enough to reliably trigger all four relays and maintain a WiFi connection simultaneously.
